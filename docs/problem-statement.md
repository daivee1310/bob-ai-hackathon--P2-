# Problem Statement

## Background

this problem belongs to the pharmaceutical and biotechnology industry, specifically the areas of drug safety, pharmacovigilance, clinical research and regulatory affairs.
## The Problem

FDA's FAERS database has 20M+ adverse event reports. Vioxx caused 27,000+ heart
attacks before its signal was acted on. Separately, a drug approval CTD dossier spans
100,000+ pages across 5 modules — one missing section gets it rejected, costing 6–12
months and $50–100M. Both problems share the same root cause: too much complex
data for manual review.


## Why It Matters

Patient safety: Delayed detection can allow serious adverse drug reactions.
Time loss: Regulatory rejection can delay approval by 6–12 months.
Financial loss: One rejected submission can cost $50–100 million.
Huge workload: FAERS has 20M+ reports and CTD dossiers can exceed 100,000 pages.
## Why Existing Solutions Fall Short

Manual review: Experts manually analyze adverse-event reports and large regulatory dossiers, which is slow and error-prone.
Basic software tools: Existing tools often handle only one task—either safety signals or document checking.
Too much data: Millions of reports and 100,000+ page dossiers are difficult to review completely.
Limited early detection: Important safety signals or missing submission sections can be overlooked.
Therefore, an integrated AI solution is needed to detect safety risks and check regulatory readiness quickly and accurately.
